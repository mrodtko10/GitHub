What This Dashboard Shows

A plain-English orientation before you dive in

The Oil & Gas Executive Dashboard tracks financial performance across active construction and pipeline projects. It pulls monthly cost report data — covering contract revenue, profitability, billing status, and earned value — and presents it in one place so you can spot trends, compare jobs, and identify problems without opening individual spreadsheets.

Data is refreshed by running the Excel macro, which overwrites the dashboard file with the latest export. The date range shown in the top-right corner of the dashboard reflects the most recent month in the data.

Note: The dashboard shows the data as of the last refresh. If something looks stale, ask whoever manages the workbook to re-run the macro and send you the updated HTML file.

The Data Snapshot

How the dashboard selects which rows to show

Each project appears once per report month in the underlying data. When you open the dashboard without any filters set, most views default to the most recent month's snapshot for each project — so a project that has been running since 2022 still shows only one row: its latest figures.

The Month filter lets you pin everything to a specific reporting period instead, which is useful for reviewing a closed month or comparing against a prior period.

Trend charts (on the Overview and Job Performance tabs) always show all months in the selected date range regardless of the Month filter.

Using the Filter Bar

All filters stack — each one narrows the data further

Section
Splits the portfolio by cost report source file — Sec. 100 or Sec. 200. Use this first if you're responsible for only one section and don't want the other section's projects mixed in. Multi-select: you can show both sections simultaneously or isolate one.
Month
Pins all snapshot views to a specific reporting month. Leave blank to see each project's most recent data. Single-select. Trend charts always span the full date range.
Status
Filters by the project's latest known status: Open (active), Closed (work complete, pending final billing), or Finalized (books closed). Multi-select. Default shows all three.
Client
Filter to one or more specific clients. Client names are extracted from the project name field (everything before the "/" separator). Multi-select. Populated from the data — only clients that exist appear here.
Start Year
Filters by the year a project was awarded, derived from the job number prefix (e.g. job 23-1002 started in 2023). Multi-select. Useful for separating newer backlog from legacy jobs.
Job #
Drill into a single job across all tabs. Once selected, every chart, KPI, and table reflects only that project. Multi-select, but most useful when narrowed to one job for detailed review.
Metric
Applies a numeric threshold filter across the portfolio — for example, show only jobs where Margin on Rev ≥ 10%, or EAC between $5M and $20M. Choose a field, an operator (≥, ≤, or Between), then enter your value. Leave blank to disable.
Reset all filters: Click the ✕ Reset button at the right end of the filter bar to clear everything at once.

Filter Tips

Common workflows

Monthly close review
Set Month to the period you just closed. Every KPI and scorecard will reflect that month's figures only — useful for verifying numbers match the cost report.
Section lead review
Select your section under Section first, then set Status → Open. You'll see only your active jobs, with no closed or finalized work inflating the numbers.
Single job deep-dive
Pick the job under Job #, then step through each tab. The Job Performance tab's month-over-month trend and scorecard are especially useful for tracking a single job's trajectory.
Find underperforming jobs
Set Metric → Margin on Rev ≤ 0. The dashboard will surface only jobs running at a loss. Cross-reference on the Project Health tab's scorecard.

Color Coding

Every color encodes the same meaning consistently across all tabs

Status badges

Open
Project is active — work in progress and costs accumulating.
Closed
Work is complete but final billing or retention may still be outstanding.
Finalized
Job is fully closed — all billing settled and books reconciled.

Margin badges

Margin badges appear on every row in the Job Scorecard and Project Scorecard tables.

+12.4%
Healthy margin — 8% or above on revenue. Green = performing at or above bid expectations.
+4.1%
Thin margin — between 0% and 8%. Yellow = below target. Watch for cost growth or CO slippage.
–6.2%
Loss position — margin on revenue is negative. Red = job is losing money. Requires PM attention.

KPI Tiles

The summary row at the top of each tab — always reflects your current filter state

Key terms

Total Portfolio Revenue
Sum of Total Projected Revenue across all filtered projects. This is the contract value, not what has been earned or billed yet.
Total Profit
Current month profit across the portfolio — revenue minus costs for the period. Can be negative if jobs are running over budget.
Blended Margin
Total profit ÷ total revenue. The portfolio-wide margin percentage, weighted by job size.
JTD Costs
Job-to-date costs — everything spent on the project from day one through the current month.
EAC
Estimate at Completion — the current forecast of total cost to finish the job. Compare to Total Revenue to assess profitability outlook.
ETC
Estimate to Complete — cost remaining from today to project close. ETC = EAC − JTD Costs.
Earned Revenue
Revenue recognized based on % complete × contract value. Differs from Billed — you can earn revenue before or after you invoice.
Outstanding A/R
Billed minus Collected. The amount invoiced but not yet received in cash.
Contingency
Reserved budget for unforeseen costs. A declining contingency balance across months is a warning sign.
Movement
Month-over-month change in profit. A positive number means the job improved; negative means it deteriorated.

Tab-by-Tab Guide

What each tab is for and what to look at first

1
Executive Overview
The starting point. Shows portfolio-level KPIs, a Revenue & Profit Trend line chart, a Portfolio Revenue Mix donut, and a Margin Distribution histogram.
  • The trend chart spans the date range you set — use it to spot months where profit dropped and investigate further.
  • The revenue mix donut shows your top 8 jobs by contract size. Everything else rolls into "Other."
  • The margin distribution histogram shows how many jobs sit in each margin band — a cluster of bars in the red/yellow zone is a portfolio-level signal.
  • The Current vs. Previous Month bar chart lets you pick any metric and see how each job moved month over month.
2
Job Performance
Per-job detail. The Job Scorecard table lists every project with margin badge, % complete, billed, and movement. Click a row (or use the Job # filter) to load the single-job drill-down panel below the table.
  • The drill-down shows the job's trend over time and a month-by-month detail table.
  • The Bid vs. Current Profit chart compares what was estimated at award versus where the job sits today — a shrinking bar means the job is burning through its margin.
  • Sort the scorecard table by any column to quickly rank jobs by margin, revenue, or % complete.
3
Project Health
Focuses on cost exposure and completion risk. The ETC Breakdown chart shows how much of each job's remaining budget is contingency versus actual work remaining.
  • The EAC vs. Revenue chart flags jobs where the Estimate at Completion is approaching or exceeding contract revenue — these are your loss candidates.
  • The Project Scorecard adds EAC, ETC, contingency, and earned margin columns alongside the margin badge.
  • A job with high EAC relative to revenue and low contingency remaining is a double warning sign.
4
Cash & Billing
Tracks the gap between work performed, invoices sent, and cash received.
  • Billing Progress by Job shows each job's % billed vs % complete — a job that is far ahead of billing may have a future cash inflow; one that is behind may need an invoice pushed.
  • The A/R Outstanding by Job bar chart ranks jobs by uncollected receivables.
  • The Cash Flow & % Complete chart tracks how cash position moves as a project progresses.
  • Collection Rate (Total Collected ÷ Total Billed) is in the KPI row — below 90% across the portfolio is worth investigating.
5
Preliminary WIP
Earned value metrics — the accounting team's view of recognized revenue and margin before the books are formally closed.
  • JTD Earned Revenue is cumulative recognized revenue based on % complete. Compare to Total Billed to understand over/under-billing.
  • Monthly Δ Contingency (highlighted red in the KPI row) shows how much contingency was consumed this month. Negative = contingency was drawn down.
  • The Earned Revenue — Cumulative & Incremental chart shows both the running total and each month's slice — useful for spotting months where recognized revenue spiked or dropped.
  • The WIP Scorecard table includes earned margin, remaining margin, and % complete side by side for every job.
